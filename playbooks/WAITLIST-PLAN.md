- **Live count: 567** (from the public `GET https://www.quit9to5.in/api/waitlist/count`). Earlier figures: 405 (Feb 2026), 530+ (May 2026). So about **+35 in 5 months**, or roughly 7 a month. The plan's target is 1,500–2,000 by launch week, which needs about **100+ a week** from now.

  - "567 people already waiting".
  - "Coming Soon".
  - An email box and a "Join the Waitlist" button.
  - "Can't wait? Book a 1:1 call here" (Topmate).
  - A Blog tab that shows blog.quit9to5.in inside the page.

### Friction and leaks found
2. **No link preview.** `<title>` is just "Quit 9to5", and there's no meta description and no og:title, og:image or og:description. Links shared on WhatsApp, LinkedIn or X show a blank card. For a page that every channel points to, this hurts a lot.
