## The one idea
A startup's runway is months of cash at its spend rate. Your plan to quit uses the same number: savings divided by monthly spend — how many months you can say no to a job you hate.

## Instagram caption (no link)

```
Your plan to quit has a runway, the same way a startup does.

A startup's runway is how many months its cash will last at the rate it spends money. Yours is the same sum: savings divided by what you spend in a month. That's how many months you can say no to a job you hate.

Example, not a target: ₹6 lakh saved and ₹50,000 a month to live is 12 months of runway. Your numbers will be different. Download the last three months of transactions, take a monthly average, and do the division.

You grow the number by saving more, or by spending less. I had about three to four years of runway when I quit. You don't need a lifetime of savings to leave.

Educational, not financial advice.

My book Quit 9to5 is coming out soon. Join the waitlist in bio.
```
