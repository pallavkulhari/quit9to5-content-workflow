Want to quit your 9to5? Then the most important number for you is your runway.

Startups use runway to estimate how long they can keep going before they run out of cash. It's the total money in the bank divided by how much they burn in a year. The same number works for your life too: your savings, divided by what you spend in a year.

Here's an example, not a target. Say you've saved ₹12 lakh and you spend ₹6 lakh a year. That gives you two years of runway. Two years where you don't need a salary, to build something of your own or to walk away from a job you hate.

How to calculate yours:
→ Add up the savings you could actually live on.
→ Download your last three months of bank and card statements, work out your average monthly spend, and multiply it by twelve.
→ Divide the first number by the second. That's your runway in years.

You can grow it in two ways: save more, or spend less. Spending less helps twice, because you save more every month and you need less for every year of runway.

When I quit, I had a runway of roughly three years.

So before you quit, don't ask "do I have enough?" Ask "how long is my runway?"
