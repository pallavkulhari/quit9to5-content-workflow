Want to quit your 9to5? The most important number is your runway.

Startups divide the money in the bank by what they burn in a year. Do the same with your life: your savings, divided by what you spend in a year.

Example, not a target: ₹12 lakh saved and ₹6 lakh spent a year is two years of runway. Two years without needing a salary.

Educational, not financial advice.
