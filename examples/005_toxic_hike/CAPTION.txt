You get a job offer with a 50 per cent salary hike. But former employees warn you the culture is toxic. Would you take it?

Most people would, in a heartbeat. On the one-dimensional career ladder, that hike screams growth.

The trouble is that a career is not one line. It has many dimensions: income, title, freedom, colleagues, location, work-life balance, toxicity, and more. Our brain can't picture all of them at once, so it takes a shortcut. It shrinks the whole career into one number. Salary. Or, at best, into two or three easy-to-flaunt ones: salary, title and company.

That's how people end up chasing the wrong metric, and optimising for the wrong life.

When I quit my 9to5, people said I was ruining my career. As if I had given everything up. I hadn't. I simply reduced one dimension, income, so I could increase another: free time.

So next time an offer screams growth, ask: growth in which dimension?
