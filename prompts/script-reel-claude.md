Before writing, read the playbook, the skill and the rules. Where the skill and the playbook disagree, follow the playbook and its 21 Nov 2026 corrections.

Write it the way reels 004 and 005 are written; Pallav called them "perfect" and wants future scripts like them:
1. **Hook from the idea itself.** Only open with "Want to quit your 9to5?" if this topic genuinely leads there. No greeting, no "in this video".
2. **Explain it out loud**, as Pallav would to a younger cousin: coherent full sentences, "Let me tell you about…". Define every concept before you apply it. No clipped fragment lines.
3. **One idea, one chain of thought.** Each line answers the question the previous line raised.
4. **One open loop**, set early and paid off after about 70% of the runtime. One line someone would forward.
5. **At most two spoken numbers**, rechecked; other figures on screen only, labelled EXAMPLE. If there are money numbers, add the on-screen fine print "Educational, not financial advice."
6. **One first-person proof line** only if the source supports it. Never invent a story, quote or statistic, and never imply something happened to Pallav that the sources don't say.
7. **Payoff, then the CTA as the last line, word for word:** "My book Quit 9to5 is coming out soon. Join the waitlist in bio."
8. British spelling, Indian context (₹, lakh, salary, hike, EMI, manager). No em dashes. No banned patterns ("X, not Y", "Here's the thing", AI vocabulary).
9. Length like the references: roughly 100–145 spoken words (004 is 135 words / 46 s; 005 is 143 words / 58 s). Every line must earn its place.
