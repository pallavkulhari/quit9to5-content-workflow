# Captions: Instagram, LinkedIn, Threads

Every reel, card and carousel needs captions. The reel captions are drafted with the script (step 2 of [reels.md](reels.md)) and reworked to match the approved voiceover before scheduling. Prompt: [prompts/caption-pack.md](../prompts/caption-pack.md).

Canonical examples: [examples/004_runway/CAPTION_v2.txt](../examples/004_runway/CAPTION_v2.txt) + [THREADS.txt](../examples/004_runway/THREADS.txt); [examples/005_toxic_hike/CAPTION.txt](../examples/005_toxic_hike/CAPTION.txt) + [THREADS.txt](../examples/005_toxic_hike/THREADS.txt).

## Instagram and LinkedIn (one shared caption for reels)

The same text goes to Instagram and the LinkedIn Page.

Structure (as in 004 and 005):
1. **Line 1 restates the idea as a claim or the reel's hook.** The caption preview is a second hook. ("Want to quit your 9to5? Then the most important number for you is your runway.")
2. **Explain more fully than the reel could.** Define the concept, in Pallav's explaining voice, full sentences.
3. **Add value the video had no room for:** the worked example in full ("Here's an example, not a target…"), or a "How to calculate yours:" list of 2–3 practical steps with `→` arrows, or the nuance ("Growth discomfort is different…").
4. **One first-person line** if the source supports it ("When I quit, I had a runway of roughly three years.").
5. **Close with a question or reframe** the reader keeps ("So before you quit, don't ask 'do I have enough?' Ask 'how long is my runway?'").
6. **"Educational, not financial advice."** on its own line if there are money numbers.
7. **The CTA as the last line:** "My book Quit 9to5 is coming out soon. Join the waitlist in bio."

Rules:
- Plain text, short paragraphs, no hashtags, no link in reel captions, no engagement bait.
- British spelling ("50 per cent" in captions even though the voice says "fifty percent").
- Numbers must match the reel exactly (004's caption was reworked to the yearly framing, ₹12 lakh and ₹6 lakh a year, once the voiceover changed).
- Every claim sourced; no new claims beyond what the reel and its sources support.

## Threads

- **500 characters maximum.** 004's Threads text is about 440 characters; 005's about 460.
- Compress the caption: hook line, the core explanation in 2–3 short paragraphs, the example (one line), the caveat if needed, the CTA.
- Keep the CTA exactly; it is the last line.
- Count characters before scheduling (Buffer rejects longer posts, and Threads truncates).

## Cards and image posts

- Cards: the caption adds something the card doesn't say and ends on an action or payoff ("Open last month's bank statement tonight…"). See [carousels-and-cards.md](carousels-and-cards.md).
- Waitlist link follows the link-in-5 counter, plain `https://quit9to5.in` on LinkedIn and Threads.

## Substack Note from a reel caption

See [substack-notes.md](substack-notes.md). Usually the Threads text or a trimmed caption, text-only, scheduled at the same slot.

## Review

Each caption goes to Pallav in **its own message** (Instagram/LinkedIn, then Threads, then the Note). Nothing is scheduled until he says "schedule".

## Checklist

- [ ] Line 1 works alone as a hook
- [ ] Explains the concept fully, in full sentences
- [ ] Adds a worked example or practical steps beyond the reel
- [ ] Numbers identical to the reel and rechecked; example labelled
- [ ] "Educational, not financial advice." when numbers appear
- [ ] Ends with the exact CTA
- [ ] Threads version 500 characters or fewer
- [ ] No hashtags, links, em dashes or banned patterns
